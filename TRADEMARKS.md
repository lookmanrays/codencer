fork, and use the open-source core commercially. It does not grant trademark,
service mark, logo, domain, or official service identity rights.

The following names, marks, domains, and service identities are protected:

- Codencer
- Codencer Cloud
- Codencer Gateway
- Official Codencer MCP

- Codencer logos and visual marks
- codencer.dev
- mcp.codencer.dev

## Allowed Uses

You may make truthful nominative references such as:

- "based on Codencer"
- "compatible with Codencer"
- "uses the Codencer open-source core"
- "forked from Codencer"

These uses must be accurate, limited to describing compatibility or origin, and
must not imply official endorsement, certification, operation, or ownership by
the Codencer project.

## Not Allowed Without Written Permission

You may not:

- name a fork, hosted service, connector, app, extension, marketplace listing,
  package, or distribution "Codencer";
- publish an app, connector, or hosted service under the Codencer name;
- use Codencer logos, official domains, or confusingly similar domains;
- imply that a fork, connector, Gateway, Cloud, MCP endpoint, or hosted service
  is official or endorsed by Codencer;
- use "Official Codencer MCP", "Codencer Gateway", or "Codencer Cloud" for a
  service you operate independently.

Forks and hosted services must use their own name and must clearly state that
they are independent unless they have written authorization.
